# Cessna172 (`source.glb`, and `../exterior.glb` and the cabin of `../cockpit.glb` made from it)

- Author: ioai25312 — https://sketchfab.com/ioai25312
- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cessna172-d1b15841c29c43d0862667300bad55a4
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 — http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`../exterior.glb` is `source.glb` moved to the aircraft's axes, scaled to the real span, its
pieces made the parts the game moves, by `import.py` (Blender).

`../cockpit.glb` is `panel.glb` (the G1000 panel and its glare shield, made for the game) inside `source.glb`'s lining,
seats, belts and glass, by `cockpit.py` (Blender).
